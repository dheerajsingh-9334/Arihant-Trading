Comprehensive blueprint documenting every interactive button, modal action, visibility condition, and dual-control approval gate across all 11 modules of Arihant BOS. Built to enforce role-based segregation of duties between Management, Sales, Tenders, Depot Fleet, Service Engineers, and Finance.

🔗 How All Modules Are Connected With Each Other Operational Data Flow

Arihant BOS connects five operational cycles together so that no action occurs in isolation. Customer interactions trigger fleet deployments, field visits directly substantiate expense vouchers, and tender milestones dictate task blockers.

1. Customer Tour & Fiscal Settlement Flow Visits ➔ Expenses
Ensures 100% voucher accountability against genuine client tours.
  • 1 Sales Schedules Field Visit
    Executive plans tour to ITBP Chandigarh or CRPF Kadarpur on /visits.
  • 2 Manager "Also-Meet" Directive
    Regional Manager attaches intervention instructions (e.g. "Also meet DIG Provisioning").
  • 3 1-Click Expense Claim Linking
    After the visit, clicking [Claim Expense] generates a claim pre-linked to visit_id.
  • 4 Two-Stage Dual Approval
    Stage 1: RM signs off subordinate's travel. Stage 2: Accounts releases NEFT with UTR code.
2. GeM Bids & Commercial Award Flow Tenders ➔ Tasks
Guarantees management review before committing financial EMD guarantees.
  • 1 GeM Scrape & Identification
    Tender team enters requirement specs, EMD fees, and bid deadline on /tenders.
  • 2 Participation Signoff Gate
    Management verifies OEM margins. Creator cannot sign off their own submission.
  • 3 Action Deliverables & EMD Task
    Tender automatically links to /tasks to track Bank Guarantee release before deadline.
  • 4 Submission & Outcome Intelligence
    Tender Team marks bid submitted. Post-opening analytics records Won/Lost with competitor stats.
3. Depot Fleet Reservation & Trial Analysis Leads ➔ Demos
Prevents equipment double-booking across Patna, Delhi, and Kolkata depots.
  • 1 Demonstration Requested
    Sales logs trial demand for NSG or State Police on /demos or during visit debrief.
  • 2 Depot Inventory Reservation
    Demo Specialist books specific unit (S/N tagged) from Delhi Depot, locking availability status.
  • 3 Field Trial Execution
    Live tactical firing or surveillance trial conducted at client shooting range.
  • 4 Trial Outcome & Root-Cause Failure Data
    Team logs officer sentiment. If failed, mandatory failure root-cause analysis is captured.
4. Equipment Breakdown & Maintenance Desk Orders ➔ Service
Ensures warranty verification and client sign-off on replacement spares.
  • 1 Incident / Defect Ticket Logged
    Emergency breakdown call logged with S/N and priority (e.g. Critical Crash Barrier failure).
  • 2 Service Engineer Dispatched
    Assigned to regional service depot engineer with planned site inspection date.
  • 3 Rectification & Spares Consumed
    Engineer replaces hydraulic valves, flushes fluid, and enters consumed spare kit numbers.
  • 4 Officer Sign-Off & Ticket Closure
    Filing formal report requires client officer confirmation before ticket marked resolved.

🎛️ Complete Button Directory: What, Why & When Action Catalog

Every button in Arihant BOS explained with its exact operational rationale, visibility logic, and backend authorization endpoint.

🛡️ Participation Signoff Tender Commercial Approval Button
Page: /tenders ➔ Inspect Modal
What It Does

Opens the Management Tender Decision modal allowing the CEO or Regional Manager to formally Approve or Reject company participation in a published government tender.

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

Defence tenders require Bank Guarantees (EMD) and strict OEM authorization letters. Without management vetting, sales reps could commit the firm to non-viable specs or unapproved margins.

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)

Shown only when tender status is awaiting_approval or under_preparation. Strictly hidden or replaced if the current user is the tender creator.

Security & RBAC Enforcement

managementregional_manager
POST /api/tenders/:id/approve
assertNotSelfApproval enforced

⚠️ Self-Approval Prohibited (Independent Review Required) Dual-Control Anti-Self-Signoff Guard
Page: /tenders ➔ Inspect Modal (When user is owner)
What It Does

Replaces the active "Participation Signoff" button with an inactive warning badge that explains why the user cannot click approve.

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

Prevents conflicts of interest. Even if Rajiv Arihant (CEO) or Vikram Sharma (RM) authored the tender draft, corporate governance requires an independent officer to sign off on participation.

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)

Appears automatically whenever user.id === tender.tender_owner_id. Informs the creator that their submission is safely logged but awaits dual sign-off.

Security & RBAC Enforcement

Frontend UI matches backend assertNotSelfApproval(user.id, existing.tender_owner_id) which returns 403 Forbidden.

✅ Mark Bid as Submitted Bid Submission Confirmation
Page: /tenders ➔ Inspect Modal
What It Does

Advances tender state from under_preparation to submitted, stamping the tender team officer's name and upload timestamp.

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

Signals to executive leadership that the commercial bid documents, technical OEM sheets, and EMD receipts have been successfully uploaded to the GeM portal before deadline.

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)

Visible only when the tender has already received participation approval and is currently in under_preparation.

🏆 Record Won / Lost Post-Bid Intelligence Capture
Page: /tenders ➔ Inspect Modal
What It Does

Opens the bid evaluation modal to log whether Arihant won or lost, winning contract value in Lakhs, winning competitor name, and reason for loss (e.g. L1 price undercut or technical QR).

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

Powers executive win/loss analytics so Arihant can adjust pricing strategies against known defence competitors (MKU, SMPP, Big Bang Boom, etc.).

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)

Visible on submitted tenders awaiting financial bid opening.

Security & RBAC Enforcement

managementtender_teamadmin

🛡️ Stage-1: RM Signoff Operational Expense Verification
Page: /expenses ➔ Claims List
What It Does

Allows the Regional Manager to verify subordinate travel vouchers against field tour itineraries, adding verification remarks and advancing claim status to manager_approved.

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

Accounts cannot verify whether a sales rep actually travelled to a border outpost. The Regional Manager is the operational supervisor who verifies the trip took place.

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)

Shown only on claims in submitted status when the viewer is NOT the submitter (user.id !== exp.employee_id).

Security & RBAC Enforcement

regional_managermanagement
PATCH /api/expenses/:id/manager-approve
assertNotSelfApproval strictly enforced

💳 Stage-2: Settle Claim Finance Disbursal & Voucher Entry
Page: /expenses ➔ Claims List
What It Does

Opens Finance Disbursement modal where Accounts records the NEFT UTR number, petty cash voucher number, or Tally ledger reference, marking the claim accounts_processed.

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

Enforces fiscal segregation of duties: managers authorize the operational validity of the expense, but ONLY Accounts can release money and log bank transactions.

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)

Visible only after a claim has passed Stage 1 (status is manager_approved).

Security & RBAC Enforcement

accountsmanagement
PATCH /api/expenses/:id/accounts-process

Attaches an executive command banner directly to an upcoming sales tour (e.g. "Meet DIG Operations regarding border camera trials") that the travelling officer must execute.

⚠️ Flag Blocker Bottleneck Escalation
Page: /tasks ➔ Task Row
What It Does

Halts milestone progress and marks task status as blocked. Collects categorized cause: vendor, customer, portal, mgmt_approval, technical, leave.

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

Government tender deadlines are fatal if missed. If an OEM hasn't sent a test certificate, the employee must flag the blocker immediately so executives can intervene before the bid window closes.

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)

Shown on any active task that is not already completed or blocked.

Security & RBAC Enforcement

All Roles
POST /api/tasks/:id/blockers

🛡️ Resolve Blocker Management Bottleneck Resolution
Page: /tasks ➔ Task Row
What It Does

Opens resolution modal to issue executive directive, select decision (accepted, extended, rejected, escalated), and optionally grant a revised target deadline.

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

Only managers have the authority to extend deadlines or authorize deviations from original project plans.

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)

Appears exclusively when a milestone is currently in blocked status.

Security & RBAC Enforcement

managementregional_manageradmin
PATCH /api/tasks/:id/blockers/:blockerId/resolve

Page: /demos ➔ Equipment Fleet Matrix

Locks a specific depot unit (serial number tagged) in Delhi, Patna, or Kolkata for a client demonstration, preventing concurrent bookings on the same trial date.

Why We Do It (Business Rationale)

High-end military hardware (thermal scopes, drone jammers) is scarce. Reserving units ensures gear is packed, calibrated, and in transit in time for the trial.

Why We Show It (Visibility Rules)
